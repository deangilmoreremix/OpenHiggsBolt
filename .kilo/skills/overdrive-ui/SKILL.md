---
name: overdrive-ui
description: Push interface design past conventional limits
---

Use when breaking design conventions purposefully.

Push boundaries:
- **Typography scale** — Extreme contrast, unusual hierarchy
- **Color saturation** — Bold, unexpected palettes that work
- **Layout innovation** — Asymmetric, diagonal, experimental grids
- **Motion sophistication** — Complex choreography, 3D transforms
- **Interaction novelty** — Gestures, multi-step reveals

Constraints still apply:
- Must be accessible (contrast, keyboard nav)
- Must be performant (no jank)
- Must serve purpose (not just experimental)

The effect should feel intentional, not accidental. Every bold choice needs justification.
