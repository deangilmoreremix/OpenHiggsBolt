# Monetization Tiers by Follower Count

> **Time:** about 18 minutes. **You will finish with:** a filled-in sponsor media kit, a bundled offer and a ready-to-send brand pitch.

---

## Before you start

Famous virtual influencers such as Lil Miquela (millions of followers, $8,000+ per post) make creators assume they cannot earn until 100k followers. In reality **B2B virtual influencers can monetize under 10,000 followers**: software buyers, accountants and developers are high-value audiences, so a tech channel with 5,000 targeted followers can command rates that entertainment channels need 100,000 for. Potential grows in three tiers:

```
Tier 3: Brand Ambassador (25K+ followers) ──► Corporate Retainers ($1,000 - $3,000/mo)
Tier 2: Dedicated Integrations (5K - 25K followers) ──► Sponsored Video Batches ($300 - $800)
Tier 1: Affiliate & SaaS referral (0 - 5K followers) ──► High-Ticket recurring commissions
```

Brands do not buy views; they buy access to demographics. They also like avatars: zero real-world PR risk, precise brief following and revisions in minutes. You need: the [sponsor media kit template](templates/sponsor-media-kit.md), your character, and SmartVideo GO AI's **AI Influencer Studio** and **Storyboard / Campaign Planner** later for production.

## Step by step

### 1. Draft your virtual media kit

Open [`templates/sponsor-media-kit.md`](templates/sponsor-media-kit.md). Fill in character profile, niche, follower metrics and target demographics. Keep the style modern and visual.

**You should see:** a kit that proves your audience matches a brand's buyers.

**If not:** if you have no real numbers yet, use clearly labelled mock figures for practice, and never present them to a brand as real.

### 2. Find advertisers with budgets

Search your niche keywords on the **Meta Ad Library**. A software brand running multiple paid social ads has an active marketing budget.

**You should see:** a list of at least three brands running paid ads in your niche.

**If not:** try broader or neighbouring keywords.

### 3. Bundle your placements

Do not pitch "one video". Offer **The Integration Pack**: 3 dedicated reels showing how your avatar uses the software, plus a pinned comment link for 30 days, priced at **$400-$600** for micro-channels.

**You should see:** a package with deliverables and a price written in your media kit.

**If not:** if the price feels arbitrary, anchor it inside the $400-$600 range above.

### 4. Write the pitch

Find the Partnership Manager's email on LinkedIn or Apollo and adapt this pitch:

```text
Hi [Name], I manage [Avatar Name], a virtual tech creator with over [Number] followers. Our audience is highly targeted around [Niche]. We love [Product Name] and would love to showcase a dashboard walkthrough to our viewers next month. Here is our media kit: [Link]. Are you currently open to short-form integration partnerships?
```

**You should see:** a pitch with every bracket replaced by real details.

**If not:** if you cannot find an email, use direct messaging for smaller founder-led brands, knowing messages often land in spam folders.

### 5. Set up tracking

After a deal, build the video to the brand guidelines and set up a tracking link (for example via Bitly or Rewardful) to measure the traffic your avatar drives.

**You should see:** a tracking link for the campaign.

**If not:** if the link shows no clicks, test it yourself before sending it to the brand.

### 6. Plan for renewal

Over-deliver on the first deal: if a brand pays for 3 videos, deliver 4 (about $0.70 more in API credits). Seven days after the campaign ends, send a clean screenshot of views, likes and link clicks.

**You should see:** a note in your calendar to deliver the extra video and send the report.

**If not:** if you forget the report, send it late; brands rarely get proactive reporting and it still stands out.

## Check yourself

* [ ] My media kit lists niche, metrics, packages and prices.
* [ ] I found three brands running paid ads.
* [ ] My pitch is personalised and links to the kit.
* [ ] I have a tracking and reporting plan.

## Common mistakes

* Waiting for 100k followers before pitching.
* Pitching "one video" instead of a bundle.
* Pitching brands with no ad activity.
* Not reporting results after the campaign.

## Go further

**Worked example, Emma (tech niche, 8,000 followers) and "TaskFlow AI":** a **$400** deal for 3 dedicated vertical clips. Production: master portrait face swapped onto a scene with Emma holding a phone, a screen recording overlay of TaskFlow's site, voiceover "Here is how I automate my daily work schedule...". Costs: API credits **$2.40**; gross **$400.00**; net **$397.60** (99% margin).

**Compare tools:**

| Tool | Strength | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Bento.me / Linktree dynamic media kits | Fast, mobile-friendly pages with in-app view stats | Showing your avatar's portfolio and stats |
| Apollo.io / Lemlist | Contact sourcing and email sequences; watch bounce rates | Cold outreach to marketing managers at scale |
| Direct social messaging | Direct access but often lands in spam requests | Small founder-led startups |

A free Bento.me link with your best vertical clips and follower stats is a professional portfolio; use Apollo for direct corporate emails rather than DMs.
