# AI Content Factories

> **Time:** about 2 hours across seven short lessons. **You will finish with:** a repeatable pipeline, vertical and loop-ready videos, thumbnails, a 30-day queue and a retainer offer.

## What this track covers

Bulk content generation, scripting batches, automated pipelines and recurring social retainers. Every lesson is a short guided walkthrough. You can produce everything in SmartVideo GO AI using its **Image Studio**, **Video Studio**, **AI Influencer Studio**, **Storyboard / Campaign Planner** and **Lip-Sync Studio**. Reusable templates live in [`templates/`](../templates/).

## Outcomes

What you will be able to do after each lesson:

## 1. Production pipeline (two parts)

A factory is built on stations, not tasks. Standardize folders, separate assembly into one-way stations, and audit exports against platform specs. Templates: `pipeline-flowchart-template.md`, `asset-specs-checklist.md`.

## 2. TikTok/Reels factory

Attention is won in the first half-second. Use 9:16 layouts with continuous motion, 1-3 word captions, and safe-zone placement. Template: `tiktok-reels-editor-preset.md`.

## 3. YouTube Shorts factory

A video that never ends is watched forever. Write loop sentences, trim tail silence, and lock audio levels. Template: `shorts-looping-blueprint.md`.

## 4. Thumbnail design

The thumbnail gets the click. Generate backgrounds with `nano-banana-2` (16:9 and 1:1), use rule-of-thirds layouts, and check readability at coin size. Template: `thumbnail-prompt-library.md`.

## 5. Batching and scheduling

Consistency is scheduled. Plan temporal batches, queue monthly runs in native studios, and repurpose long videos with clipping tools. Template: `30-day-production-calendar.md`.

## 6. Selling as a service

Businesses want consistency, not views. Package retainers, write proposals with revision caps, and bill upfront with Stripe links. Templates: `agency-retainer-proposal.md`, `bulk-pricing-sheet.md`.
