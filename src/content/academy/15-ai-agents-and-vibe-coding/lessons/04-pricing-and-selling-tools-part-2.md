# Pricing & Selling Tools You Build With Agents, part 2

> **Time:** about 12 minutes. **You will finish with:** a live product listing, a launch post for one community and an affiliate offer.

---

## Before you start

In part 1 you set a price and wrote the page. Now you publish it and bring buyers. The **Creator Tool Monetization Stack** sells through three channels at once: a direct Gumroad or LemonSqueezy page, a creator community launch, and an affiliate partner network. Together they turn one micro-tool into passive revenue.

You will need: your product page copy and tool zip from part 1, a Gumroad or LemonSqueezy account, and about 12 minutes. Prerequisite: lesson 4, part 1.

## Step by step

### 1. Choose a platform

* **Gumroad** (gumroad.com): best for one-time purchases. 10% fee on the free plan, 0% on the $10/month paid plan. Handles global VAT automatically.
* **LemonSqueezy** (lemonsqueezy.com): best for subscriptions or a checkout embed on your own site. 5% + $0.50 per transaction.

**You should see:** one platform chosen to match your pricing model from part 1.

**If not:** fees change; check the platform's own pricing page before you decide.

### 2. Publish the product

1. Create a new product and paste in your page copy.
2. Upload your zip, set your price and publish.

**You should see:** a live product page, usually within about 10 minutes.

**If not:** if a field is unclear, check the platform's help page rather than guessing. Never put API keys in the zip.

### 3. Record a before and after demo

Record a 60-second Loom video of the tool running on real data, showing before and after. Text posts alone convert at about 5-10%; video demos convert at 20-35%.

**You should see:** a short clip showing the manual alternative versus your tool.

**If not:** if it runs long, cut it to the one transformation the buyer cares about.

### 4. Launch where your buyer already is

Post your demo in one community:

* **POD / Etsy tools:** Facebook groups ("Printify Sellers", "Etsy Print on Demand Success"), Reddit r/Etsy and relevant Discord servers.
* **AI image tools:** the Midjourney community Discord, r/StableDiffusion and AI creator newsletters.

Follow each community's rules on promotion.

**You should see:** your post live, with the video and your product link.

**If not:** if a group bans promotion, ask a moderator or choose another community.

### 5. Set up an affiliate offer

Gumroad's built-in affiliate system and LemonSqueezy both support commission referrals. Offer **30-40% commission** to creators with 1,000-10,000 followers in your niche who do not sell their own tools yet. Pitch:

```text
You mention my tool once in a newsletter or video; you earn $12 every time a subscriber buys. No ongoing work.
```

**You should see:** affiliate commission enabled and one creator contacted.

**If not:** if you cannot find the setting, search the platform's help for affiliates, since menu names change.

## Check yourself

## Common mistakes

* **Launching without a demo.** Video converts far better than text.
* **Posting everywhere at once.** Start with one community and learn.
* **No affiliates.** Other creators' audiences are your cheapest reach.
* **Shipping and forgetting.** Plan updates.
