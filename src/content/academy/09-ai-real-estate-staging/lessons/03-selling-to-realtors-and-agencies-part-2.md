# Selling to Realtors & Agencies, part 2

> **Time:** about 10 minutes. **You will finish with:** a 60-second video audit plan, a retainer offer, and a photographer partnership and compliance checklist.

---

## Before you start

Once an agent replies to your sample, you need a closer and a recurring offer. After an agent experiences a 24-hour turnaround on an active listing, lock them into a **Monthly Agency Retainer** ($499-$1,299/month) for upcoming listings. Photographers shoot 20-50 homes a month and can multiply your reach. You need part 1 finished, [`templates/agency-outreach-template.md`](templates/agency-outreach-template.md), and a Loom or Vidyard account (free tier) for the optional video.

## Step by step

### 1. Plan a 60-second video audit (optional, premium listings)

For listings of **$1M+**, script a 60-second Loom showing:

1. Their current empty Zillow listing photo.
2. Your AI staged transformation.
3. A short walk-through of the 3 style options: Scandinavian, Luxury, Coastal.

**You should see:** a three-part outline you can record in one take.

**If not:** if you skip video, a before/after email still works.

### 2. Present the Brokerage Unlimited Pass

After you deliver their first listing package, offer:

* **Package rate:** **$699/month** (up to 5 properties / 30 staged photos per month).
* **Agent benefit:** guaranteed 12-hour turnaround, priority style requests and dedicated listing video clips.

**You should see:** a short retainer offer you can paste into a follow-up email.

**If not:** if it feels too big, remember the worked example signed agents at $599/month; set your own price within $499-$1,299.

### 3. Draft a photographer partnership offer

Offer local real estate photographers a **30% white-label commission split** ($50 per house staged) to sell your staging to their existing agent clients. Use the partnership agreement in [`templates/agency-outreach-template.md`](templates/agency-outreach-template.md).

**You should see:** a short pitch to one photographer.

**If not:** pick one local photographer whose work you like and start with just that one.

### 4. Add the MLS compliance check

If local MLS board rules require it, add a subtle watermark in the bottom corner reading *"Virtually Staged for Visualization"*. Note this on your delivery checklist.

**You should see:** a delivery checklist with a compliance line.

**If not:** check your local MLS board's rules; menus and rules differ by board.

## Check yourself

* [ ] I have a 3-part video audit outline for premium listings.
* [ ] My retainer offer is written ($699/month, 5 properties / 30 photos).
* [ ] I drafted a 30% photographer split pitch.
* [ ] My delivery checklist includes the MLS watermark check.

## Common mistakes

* Pitching the retainer before delivering a first package.
* Skipping the MLS compliance check.
* Using a long video instead of 60 seconds.
