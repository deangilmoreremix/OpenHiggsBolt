# AI Filmmaking

> Short films, music videos, trailers and documentaries, made without a crew. Everything here can be produced in SmartVideo GO AI using its Image, Video, Audio, Cinema, Lip-Sync, Storyboard, VFX, Thumbnail and AI Influencer studios.

## How this track works

Each lesson is a short guided walkthrough of 14-17 minutes. You follow numbered steps, check what you should see, and finish with a concrete result. Optional extras (worked example, exercises, tool comparison, ways to earn) sit in a "Go further" section at the end of each lesson. Reusable templates are saved in [`templates/`](templates/).

## What you will be able to do

* **Screenplay:** write stories that AI video models can actually render, and turn each shot into a prompt. Template: [`screenplay-prompt-template.md`](templates/screenplay-prompt-template.md), [`ai-film-brief.md`](templates/ai-film-brief.md).
* **Storyboarding:** lock character, lighting and palette with still images before spending on video. Templates: [`shot-list-template.md`](templates/shot-list-template.md), [`style-guide-template.md`](templates/style-guide-template.md), [`storyboard-script-template.md`](templates/storyboard-script-template.md).
* **Camera movement:** use cinematography verbs, first-frame conditioning and motion strength to get cinematic shots. Templates: [`motion-prompt-library.md`](templates/motion-prompt-library.md), [`cinematography-cheat-sheet.md`](templates/cinematography-cheat-sheet.md).
* **Assembly:** cut clips, layer narration, ambience, Foley and music, and grade with a LUT. Template: [`sound-design-checklist.md`](templates/sound-design-checklist.md).
* **Selling:** package deliverables, pitch sponsors, enter festivals and license episodes. Templates: [`sponsorship-pitch-template.md`](templates/sponsorship-pitch-template.md), [`licensing-agreement-sheet.md`](templates/licensing-agreement-sheet.md).

## Where you will work in SmartVideo GO AI

* **Image Studio:** anchors, character sheets, product and brand shots.
* **Video Studio:** image-to-video motion.
* **AI Influencer Studio:** consistent recurring on-brand characters.
* **Storyboard / Campaign Planner:** batch matrices, ad briefs and test plans.
* **Lip-Sync Studio:** voice and mouth-sync for avatar performances.

The workflow is the same everywhere: Learn, See Example, Use Template, Generate.
