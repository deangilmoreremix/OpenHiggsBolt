# Scaling to Multiple Channels, part 2

> **Time:** about 15 minutes. **You will finish with:** finished vertical videos scheduled across YouTube, TikTok and Instagram with a safe posting plan.

---

## Before you start

With scripts and voices done (part 1), the rest of the batch is visuals, editing and scheduling. A 9:16 vertical video can go to YouTube Shorts (AdSense pool, subscribers), TikTok (Creator Rewards Program) and Instagram Reels (brand sponsorship reach, visual discovery) with zero extra production cost. If you also make long-form videos (8+ minutes), you can extract 5-10 short highlights from them.

You will need: your scripts and audio from part 1, a way to generate visuals (SmartVideo GO AI's Image Studio and Video Studio), an editor such as CapCut, and logins for YouTube Studio, TikTok Studio and Instagram Creator Studio. If you do this with a single video first, it takes the time stated; a full batch of five takes longer.

## Step by step

### 1. Batch-render the visuals

Open your image generators and queue prompts for all videos in one session. Save results in folders labelled `[Video_1]`, `[Video_2]` and so on.

**You should see:** a folder per video filled with its visuals.

**If not:** if a generation fails, re-run only that prompt and keep the folder naming.

### 2. Import audio and visuals into your editor

Import all audio tracks and visual folders into CapCut or your editor.

**You should see:** every video's assets available in one project.

**If not:** if files look missing, re-import from the folder rather than the download list.

### 3. Edit all videos in one sitting

Edit the videos back to back, copy-pasting caption styles and audio settings between them, then export the finished files.

**You should see:** finished 9:16 files, auto-captioned.

**If not:** if caption styles do not carry over, save one as a preset and reuse it.

### 4. Upload and schedule on each platform

Upload to YouTube Studio, TikTok Studio and Instagram Creator Studio, and use the built-in schedulers to set release times days in advance.

**You should see:** each video listed as scheduled on each platform.

**If not:** menus move; follow the current screen. If scheduling is not offered for an account, post at your planned time instead.

### 5. Apply the safety rules

Post no more than 1 upload per day per platform, and never upload the exact same video file to two different channels on the same platform.

**You should see:** a posting calendar with at most one upload per day per platform and distinct content per channel.

**If not:** spread extra videos to later days and change the content for a second channel.

## Check yourself

* [ ] My videos are exported as 9:16 files.
* [ ] They are scheduled on all three platforms using native schedulers.
* [ ] No platform gets more than 1 upload a day.
* [ ] No identical file goes to two channels on the same platform.

## Common mistakes

* Spam uploading, such as 10 videos a day on a new account, which triggers spam filters and tanks views.
* Uploading the same file to two channels. Platforms check video hashes and flag repetitive content.
* Using third-party schedulers for creator channels, which can restrict trending audio.

## Go further

**Compare tools.** Native schedulers (YouTube Studio, TikTok web): free, safe, support custom thumbnails; the default choice. Programmatic clipping ([`ai-clipping-generator`](https://github.com/Anil-matcha/awesome-generative-ai-apps/tree/main/video_generation/ai-clipping-generator)): detects speech pauses and cuts long videos into highlights. Third-party schedulers (Buffer/Later): one dashboard, but may restrict custom music or carousel formats.

**Exercises.** Medium: schedule a 5-day posting calendar with the native schedulers and log dates and times. Hard: use `ai-clipping-generator` to cut a 10-minute speech into three short clips and write a hook line for each.
