# Niche Selection & Script Pipeline

> **Time:** about 15 minutes. **You will finish with:** a graded shortlist of three niches, one chosen niche, and a filled-in script plan for your first video.

---

## Before you start

If your niche has low CPM, your view count does not matter; if your script has low retention, platforms will not show it to anyone. Generic niches such as scary campfire stories or motivational quotes often pay an RPM under $0.50 per 1,000 views, so $1,000 needs about 2 million views. The formula is `Earnings = (Views / 1000) * RPM + Direct Conversions`. In a high-CPM niche (business software, SaaS, banking, real estate, luxury travel) RPM can reach $10-$30, so at $20 RPM you need only 50,000 views to make $1,000. This part picks the niche and plans one video. Part 2 writes and audits the script.

You will need: the two templates linked in the steps, a browser, and about 15 minutes. You will use SmartVideo GO AI's Storyboard / Campaign Planner later to lay out the script; this part needs only the templates.

## Step by step

### 1. Pick three candidate niches

Write down three niches you could talk about for months. Favor industries that sell high-priced products (SaaS, banking, real estate, business software, luxury travel).

**You should see:** a list of three niche names.

**If not:** if you only have ideas like "funny moments" or "quote compilations", add at least one business or software angle, because those are the low-CPM traps.

### 2. Open the Niche Evaluation Matrix

Open [`templates/niche-evaluation-matrix.md`](templates/niche-evaluation-matrix.md) and list your three niches in it.

**You should see:** the score sheet with one row or column per niche.

**If not:** copy the template contents into a document of your own and use that.

### 3. Check advertiser density for each niche

For each niche, search its key terms in the **Meta Ad Library** or **Google Keyword Planner**. High Cost-Per-Click (CPC) bids, or dozens of brands running paid social ads for the keyword, mean advertisers are bidding heavily, so your RPM will be high.

**You should see:** a clear difference between niches: some keywords with many advertisers or high bids, some with few.

**If not:** menus move; follow the current screen. If Keyword Planner asks for setup you do not want to do, use the Meta Ad Library alone.

### 4. Score the niches and choose one

Fill in the scores in the matrix and select the niche with the highest score.

**You should see:** one winning niche. (For comparison, the worked example "Automate Smarter" in SaaS automation scored 22/25.)

**If not:** if two niches tie, pick the one with software affiliate programs, because those become your main income while you wait for the YouTube Partner Program.

### 5. Plan one video concept

Open [`templates/retention-script-template.md`](templates/retention-script-template.md). Choose a single target problem, for example "how to automate your business bookkeeping" rather than the broad "introduction to accounting". Write the topic at the top of the template.

**You should see:** one narrow topic written in the template.

**If not:** if your topic sounds like a school subject, narrow it until it names one problem and one result.

## Check yourself

* [ ] I graded three niches in the Niche Evaluation Matrix.
* [ ] I checked advertiser density for each one before scoring.
* [ ] I chose one niche with the highest score.
* [ ] My first video topic solves one narrow problem.

## Common mistakes

* Choosing a niche because it is easy to generate. Easy niches usually have low advertiser demand.
* Skipping the advertiser check. Bids and ads are the evidence for RPM.
* A broad topic. One video, one problem.

**Exercises.**

1. Easy: audit three niches with the matrix and select the highest score.
2. Medium: take a dry technical blog post and plan a 60-second script from it (finish it in part 2).
3. Hard: outline three distinct pattern interrupts (a transition sound effect, a rapid zoom-in, a text card overlay) and say where on the timeline each goes.

**Compare tools for scripting.** Claude 3.5 Sonnet or Gemini 1.5 Pro: excellent at formatting scripts, a natural tone and strict word counts, best for drafting. ChatGPT Plus (GPT-4o): good for topic brainstorming, but tends toward cheesy hooks such as "Are you tired of...". Traditional script editors suit long linear narratives, not short-form hooks.

**Monetize this step.** Check advertiser density before creating the channel, and research SaaS tools in your niche with affiliate programs, typically paying 20-30% recurring monthly commissions.
