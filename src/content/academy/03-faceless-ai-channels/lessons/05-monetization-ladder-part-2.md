# Monetization Ladder, part 2

> **Time:** about 15 minutes. **You will finish with:** an affiliate pitch sent, a three-email automated sequence drafted, and a pinned-comment call to action.

---

## Before you start

With a lead magnet, landing page and checkout in place (part 1), the money comes from the ladder above them: affiliate commissions from software you already recommend, and the digital product, both sold through automated email. A worked example: "Automate Smarter" at 2,500 subscribers and 60,000 monthly Shorts views, not yet in AdSense, converts 0.5% to 300 signups; 3% buy a $49/mo tool at 30% recurring commission ($14.70 each) for 9 sales = $132.30/month; 2% buy a $29 template for 6 sales = $174.00/month; total $306.30/month against $8.40 of production cost (12 videos at $0.70).

You will need: [`templates/affiliate-pitch-template.md`](templates/affiliate-pitch-template.md), [`templates/funnel-setup-checklist.md`](templates/funnel-setup-checklist.md), your landing page, and an email tool such as ConvertKit or MailerLite.

## Step by step

### 1. List the software you recommend

Write down the tools you show or recommend in your videos.

**You should see:** a short list of tools, ideally ones with affiliate programs (typically 20-30% recurring monthly).

**If not:** if none has a program, check each tool's website for "partners" or "affiliates".

### 2. Send affiliate pitches

Use [`templates/affiliate-pitch-template.md`](templates/affiliate-pitch-template.md) to pitch for custom referral rates, then generate your tracking links.

**You should see:** at least one pitch sent and, once approved, a tracking link.

**If not:** if there is no reply, sign up for the standard program and pitch again later with your numbers.

### 3. Draft email 1 (Day 0)

In your email tool, create the automation and write Email 1 to deliver the free lead magnet.

**You should see:** an email with the lead magnet link.

**If not:** menus move; follow the current screen. Send the email to yourself to test the link.

### 4. Draft emails 2 and 3

* Email 2 (Day 2): a high-value tip or case study related to the magnet.
* Email 3 (Day 4): introduce the premium product, showing before/after results.

Keep the tone helpful and non-spammy.

**You should see:** three emails scheduled at Day 0, Day 2 and Day 4.

**If not:** if the email reads like an ad, remove adjectives and add one concrete result.

### 5. Write the pinned comment and the in-video line

Pin a comment such as "Get the free budget sheet template here: [link]". In the video, do not spend 20 seconds selling; just say "I put the template in the comments below."

**You should see:** a pinned comment with your landing page link.

**If not:** if you cannot pin, add the link as the first line of the description.

### 6. Add the disclosure and test the funnel

Where you use affiliate links, add the disclosure from lesson 3. Then sign up through your own landing page and check the email arrives.

**You should see:** the lead magnet email in your inbox shortly after sign-up.

**If not:** check the form is connected to the right list or automation.

## Check yourself

* [ ] I sent at least one affiliate pitch.
* [ ] My three-email sequence is set for Day 0, 2 and 4.
* [ ] My pinned comment and bio link point to the landing page.
* [ ] I tested the funnel from sign-up to email.

## Common mistakes

* Hard-selling in the video. Let the email sequence sell.
* Hiding the link at the bottom of the description (a pinned comment gets about 3x more clicks).
* Forgetting affiliate disclosures.
