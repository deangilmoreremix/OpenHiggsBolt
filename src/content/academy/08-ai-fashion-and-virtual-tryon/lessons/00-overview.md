# AI Fashion & Virtual Try-On

> Virtual try-on and editorial lookbooks on demand: flat garments draped onto custom models with texture and branding preserved.

You will go from a flat garment photo to a consistent, measured, tested catalog. Each lesson is a short guided walk-through with a result at the end. You need a garment photo and SmartVideo GO AI **Image Studio**; Video Studio, AI Influencer Studio and Storyboard / Campaign Planner help in later lessons.

| # | Lesson | Time | You finish with |
|:---:|---|:---:|---|
| 1 | [Garment Try-on for Fashion E-commerce](01-garment-tryon.md) | ~16 min | A garment on a model, checked at zoom |
| 2 | [High-converting Studio Lookbooks](02-studio-lookbooks.md) | ~18 min | A graded lookbook image |
| 3 | [Sizing & Layout Consistency](03-sizing-layout-consistency.md) | ~15 min | An image aligned to the grid |
| 4 | [Before/After Conversion Cases](04-conversion-cases.md) | ~15 min | Return rate, body-type shots, callout |
| 5 | [Before/After Conversion Cases, part 2](04-conversion-cases-part-2.md) | ~10 min + 30 days | A running A/B test |

Do lessons 1 to 2 in order; lesson 3 stands alone (it expects a try-on render); lesson 4 needs lesson 1. Reusable files are in [`templates/`](templates/): `tryon-spec-sheet.md`, `lookbook-moodboard.md`, `mannequin-alignment-template.md`, `fashion-cvr-tracker.md`.

Start with: Garment Try-on for Fashion E-commerce.
