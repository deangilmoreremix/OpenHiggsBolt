# Standing Out Against Fiverr Competition

> **Time:** about 12 minutes. **You will finish with:** three priced B2B team packages and a ready intake form that matches the proposal template.

---

## Before you start

The consumer AI headshot market is commoditized: apps and Fiverr freelancers sell **$5 to $10 packs**. At that price, acquisition costs eat margins and refund requests ruin profit. Companies, law firms, startups and agencies, however, spend **$3,000 to $10,000 annually** on team headshots and will not use cheap apps because of privacy, brand inconsistency and low resolution. The move is from B2C micro-transactions to **B2B team packages**, built on four pillars: brand style guide matching, enterprise privacy (GDPR-compliant purging of source selfies within 7 days), high-resolution delivery (300dpi, 4000px+), and a self-serve intake portal.

You will need [`templates/b2b-headshot-proposal.md`](templates/b2b-headshot-proposal.md) and a form tool (Tally, Typeform or your own domain). Use SmartVideo GO AI **Storyboard / Campaign Planner** to organise the offer if you like. Menus move; follow the current screen.

## Step by step

### 1. Write your three package tiers

Copy this layout into your notes and adjust only if you have reason to:

```text
Startup Team Pass - $399
- Up to 10 Employees
- 3 Styles per Person
- Brand Background Match
- 48h Turnaround

Corporate Standard - $799
- Up to 25 Employees
- 5 Styles per Person
- Custom Brand Match
- 24h Turnaround

Enterprise Unlimited - $1,499
- Up to 50 Employees
- Custom Brand Background
- Priority 12h SLA
- GDPR Data Purge Cert
```

**You should see:** three tiers, each with a price, headcount and turnaround.

**If not:** if a tier has no clear headcount, add one; buyers compare on seats.

### 2. Open the proposal template

Open [`templates/b2b-headshot-proposal.md`](templates/b2b-headshot-proposal.md) and find the intake form schema.

**You should see:** the intake fields, proposal wording and GDPR agreement sections.

**If not:** build the form from the next step's field list instead.

### 3. Build the white-labeled intake form

1. Create a clean form in Tally, Typeform or your own domain.
2. Add these fields: employee full name and title; email for proof delivery; outfit preference (for example Executive Suit, Tech Casual Sweater, Creative Blazer); an upload field for 3 crisp selfies.

**You should see:** a form where an employee can finish in about 2 minutes from a phone.

**If not:** if uploads fail on phones, set a larger file limit or accept 3 files separately.

### 4. Add the privacy promise

1. Add a line stating all source selfies are permanently deleted within 7 business days of delivery.
2. Link the GDPR privacy agreement from the proposal template.

**You should see:** the promise visible on the form before the upload field.

**If not:** do not promise what you cannot do. Only commit to the deletion window you will actually honor.

## Check yourself

* [ ] I have three priced tiers ($399, $799, $1,499).
* [ ] My intake form has name, title, email, outfit choice and selfie upload.
* [ ] The form states the selfie deletion promise.
* [ ] I can explain why B2B beats $5 packs.

## Common mistakes

* **Selling $10 packs to individuals.** The margin never survives acquisition and refunds.
* **Vague tiers.** Buyers need headcount, styles and turnaround.
* **Promising deletion you do not do.** Privacy claims must be real.
