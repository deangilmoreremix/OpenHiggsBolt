# Standing Out Against Fiverr Competition, part 2

> **Time:** about 17 minutes. **You will finish with:** a target list, a personalised cold pitch, and a delivery folder structure for a team bundle.

---

## Before you start

With packages and an intake form ready, you need buyers and a clean delivery. High-growth companies are the best targets: they have just raised money, rebranded or hired remote staff, and their team page shows mixed photo quality. You will pitch HR heads and VP Marketing, then deliver bundles that cover web, print and chat avatars.

You will need your tiers and form from part 1, and LinkedIn Sales Navigator or Apollo (or free manual research). Use SmartVideo GO AI **Image Studio** to make the before/after sample. Menus move; follow the current screen. Never paste secrets or client passwords into chat.

## Step by step

### 1. Find 10 target companies

1. Use Sales Navigator, Apollo or manual search to list companies that recently announced a Series A/B round, rebranded their website, or hired 5+ new remote employees in the past month.
2. Note an HR Director or VP Marketing name for each.

**You should see:** a list of 10 companies with a named contact each.

**If not:** widen the filter to any company whose team page shows inconsistent photos.

### 2. Make a 30-second before/after sample

Take one public leadership photo (with permission) or your own selfie, and render a unified-brand version in **Image Studio** using lesson 1 settings.

**You should see:** a before/after image or short clip with the team-page background and lighting you would offer.

**If not:** do not use someone's photo without consent. Use your own or a friend's.

### 3. Personalise and send the pitch

Replace the bracketed fields and send:

```text
Subject: Consistent headshots for [Company Name]'s remote team?

Hi [HR Director Name],

I noticed [Company Name]'s team page features great talent, but the headshots range from professional studio shots to vacation photos and outdoor selfies.

As remote teams grow, getting 25 employees across 4 time zones into a consistent photography studio is nearly impossible.

Our studio provides Unified AI Corporate Headshots for remote teams:
* Every team member gets 5 polished, studio-quality executive portraits matching your exact company brand guidelines.
* Zero studio travel - employees upload 3 selfies from their phone in 2 minutes.
* Full GDPR compliance and automated selfie deletion after 7 days.

I put together a quick 30-second sample showing what your team page would look like with a unified brand background:

[Link to 30-Sec Sample / Before-After Image]

Would you be open to testing 2 free employee headshot samples for your leadership team this week?

Best,
[Your Name]
Founder, [Your Agency Name]
```

**You should see:** an email with no remaining brackets and a working sample link.

**If not:** if you cannot promise the 7-day deletion or 5 portraits per person, edit those lines to match what you offer.

### 4. Set up the delivery folders

Plan one folder per employee containing:

```text
john_doe_linkedin_1:1.jpg     (1080x1080px for LinkedIn/Slack)
john_doe_website_4:5.jpg      (2400x3000px for the corporate team page)
john_doe_print_300dpi.png     (high-res print transparent PNG for press releases)
```

**You should see:** a folder pattern you can repeat for every employee.

**If not:** if your system dislikes a colon in file names, use a dash instead and keep it consistent.

## Check yourself

* [ ] I have 10 target companies with contacts.
* [ ] I have a before/after sample made with permission.
* [ ] My pitch is personalised with no brackets left.
* [ ] My delivery folder lists LinkedIn, website and print files.

## Common mistakes

* **A generic pitch.** Name the company and the inconsistency you saw on its team page.
* **Using photos without consent.**
* **Delivering only web-size files.** Print needs the 300dpi PNG.

**Closing rules:** include a signed data privacy clause that source selfies are deleted within 7 business days. Offer a **$99/month** retainer covering up to 3 new hires per month.
