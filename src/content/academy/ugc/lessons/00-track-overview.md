# AI Video Ads & UGC

> **Time:** about 2 minutes to read. **You will finish with:** a clear map of six short lessons that take you from first script to a sold UGC ad service.

---

## Before you start

This track turns AI-generated video into ads brands actually pay for. You work inside the SmartVideo GO AI studios: Image, Video, Audio, Lip-Sync, Storyboard / Campaign Planner and AI Influencer. Each lesson is a short guided walkthrough with a Copy button on every prompt. Do them in order; each one builds on the last.

Reusable files (scripts, checklists, pricing sheets) live in `templates/`.

## What each lesson gives you

* **How AI UGC Actually Works (parts 1 and 2):** the 5-stage pipeline, Script → Avatar/Voice → Video → Captions → Export, ending with a captioned 9:16 ad. Templates: `templates/ugc-script-template.md`, `templates/ad-brief-checklist.md`.
* **Character & Face Consistency:** anchor an AI creator so the face stays the same across shots. Template: `templates/character-consistency-checklist.md`.
* **Building a 10-Ad Batch:** lock the constants, vary hook and angle, deliver a labelled test plan. Template: `templates/batch-matrix-template.md`.
* **Pricing & Selling UGC Ads as a Service:** gig ($10-$55/ad), project ($150-$300/batch), retainer ($1,500-$3,000+/month). Templates: `templates/outreach-template.md`, `templates/retainer-proposal-template.md`.
* **Case Study Teardown:** break a real running ad into five layers and reuse its structure. Template: `templates/teardown-worksheet.md`.

## Common mistakes

* **Skipping the consistency lesson.** A face that changes shot to shot gets ads rejected.
* **Pitching the AI process.** Lead with the finished ad and the business outcome.
