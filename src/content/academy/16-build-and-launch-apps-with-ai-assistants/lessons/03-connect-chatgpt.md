# Connect ChatGPT the same way

ChatGPT can also use connectors built on the same standard. Whether you can add them, and where, depends on your plan and on settings OpenAI changes from time to time, so check OpenAI's current help page first.

## Steps

1. Open ChatGPT and look in its settings for connectors or apps.
2. Add Netlify, Stripe and Clerk, signing in to your own accounts and approving access to those only.
3. Run the same read-only test message you used with Claude and compare the answers with your dashboards.

## Which one should I use?

You do not need both. Pick one for the build and keep the other as a second opinion on hard problems.

## If your plan does not allow connectors

Do the build with Claude, or ask the assistant to explain each change and make the dashboard changes yourself.
