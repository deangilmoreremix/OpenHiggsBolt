# Track 16: Build and Launch Apps with Claude or ChatGPT, Netlify, Clerk and Stripe

> **SmartVideo GO AI Academy** — Learn to take one of the ready-made reseller apps from your own GitHub copy to a live, hosted business, with an AI chat doing the technical work for you through connectors.

## What you will be able to do

* Open free **Netlify**, **Clerk** and **Stripe** accounts and know what each one is for.
* Connect **Claude** and/or **ChatGPT** to those services with connectors (plugins), safely.
* Brief your assistant on an app, host it on Netlify, add Clerk sign-up, connect Stripe test payments, and check the result from the chat.
* Repeat the same build for any of the 38 reseller apps.

## Lessons

1. Start your free accounts (Netlify, Clerk, Stripe)
2. Connect Claude to Netlify, Clerk and Stripe
3. Connect ChatGPT the same way
4. Build an app end to end with your assistant

The step-by-step version, with a Learn / Follow along / Complete / Verify flow and your selected app's own facts, is **Module 13** of the Reseller Setup Walkthrough (`/academy/reseller/launch`). This track is the short, plain-language overview. Open Module 13 when you are ready to build.

## What the apps already include

The reseller apps already have Stripe payments, a PostgreSQL database (through Prisma) and Google sign-in (through NextAuth). **Hosting on Netlify and signing people in with Clerk are changes your assistant makes in your own copy**, in a separate branch, with your approval at each step.

## Safety rules for the whole track

* Sign in to your own accounts only.
* Test with a read-only question before you let a chat change anything.
* **Never paste a secret key into a chat.** Secrets go into the Netlify, Clerk and Stripe dashboards. The assistant only tells you the variable names.
* Keep Stripe in test mode until you deliberately go live.
* Menu names in Claude, ChatGPT and each service change often. Follow the official page instead of an old screenshot.
