# AI Stock Content & Licensing

> Generate once, license repeatedly. Build a high-volume stock catalog that earns recurring royalties, with no client management.

You will learn what buyers actually download, how to produce a catalog in batches, and what licensing and income to realistically expect. Everything can be produced in SmartVideo GO AI with **Image Studio**, **Video Studio**, **AI Influencer Studio**, **Storyboard / Campaign Planner** and **Lip-Sync Studio**.
