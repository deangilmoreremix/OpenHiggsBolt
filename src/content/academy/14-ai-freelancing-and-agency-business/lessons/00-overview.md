# AI Freelancing and Agency Business

> **Time:** about 2 minutes to read. **You will finish with:** a clear picture of the five short lessons that take you from pricing to a sales-ready portfolio.

---

## What this track is

This track covers the business side of selling AI creation services: price by value, protect yourself with a contract, find B2B clients, delegate work, and show results as case studies. Each lesson is a short guided walkthrough. All dollar figures are examples from the course, not income guarantees.
