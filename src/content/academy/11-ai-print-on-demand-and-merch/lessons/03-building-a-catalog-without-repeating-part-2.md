# Building a Catalog Without Repeating Yourself, part 2

> **Time:** about 15 minutes. **You will finish with:** 12 isolated, upscaled graphics cross-published as 36 listings after a trademark check.

---

## Before you start

You have 12 graphics from part 1. Now you turn them into a sellable catalog: isolate and upscale in batch, apply each graphic to 3 high-margin product types, and clear trademark risk. 12 graphics x 3 products = **36 unique store listings**.

You need the 12 generated graphics, your connected supplier from lesson 2, a background remover and upscaler from lesson 1, and `templates/merch-prompt-brief.md` (trademark checklist).

## Step by step

### 1. Isolate all 12 backgrounds

Run each graphic through your AI background remover and save transparent PNGs.

**You should see:** 12 transparent PNGs with no boxes.

**If not:** redo any with leftover background; regenerate if edges stay messy.

### 2. Upscale all 12 in batch

Upscale with a 4x factor to at least 4500px wide at 300 DPI.

**You should see:** 12 crisp, print-ready files.

**If not:** rerun any that look soft, as in lesson 1.

### 3. Pick three product types

Choose three high-margin products: T-Shirt, Coffee Mug, Canvas Tote Bag.

**You should see:** three product templates ready in your supplier.

**If not:** pick the closest available product.

### 4. Cross-publish

Apply each graphic to each of the 3 products and publish to your store.

**You should see:** a path to **36 listings** (12 x 3).

**If not:** start with one graphic on all 3 products, confirm it looks right, then repeat.

### 5. Check trademarks

Never use trademarked names (for example Disney, Marvel, Nike) or celebrity likenesses in prompts or store tags. Search the USPTO TESS database before publishing, and use the checklist in `templates/merch-prompt-brief.md`.

**You should see:** no protected names in any graphic, title or tag.

**If not:** remove or rename the listing before it goes live.

## Check yourself

* [ ] All 12 graphics are transparent and upscaled.
* [ ] Each graphic is on 3 product types.
* [ ] I searched for trademark conflicts.

## Common mistakes

* Publishing without checking trademarks or celebrity likenesses.
* Publishing all 36 before checking one test listing.
* Skipping the upscale step on batch files.
