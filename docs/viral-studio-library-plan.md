## Implementation Notes

1. **Landing Page vs Viral Studio:** The landing page remains curated with 14 niche sections. Each section shows 6-12 of the best-matching templates. The Viral Studio is the massive library with all 2,457 templates accessible via industry/niche/style/objective browsing.

2. **Visibility Rules:** Niches with 8+ templates get a dedicated browse section. Niches with 3-7 templates are searchable. Niches with 1-2 templates are hidden from browse but searchable.

3. **Non-English Templates:** 4 templates (all from minimax_h3) are non-English and excluded from the default English library. They should be preserved in source inventory for potential localized sections.

4. **Duplicate Handling:** 80 seedance_2prompt records had duplicate slugs. These were deduplicated keeping the first occurrence. The canonical audit shows 2,541 raw records; the working set is 2,461 unique.

5. **Style Classification:** Templates can belong to multiple styles (up to 8 per template). The most common styles are Cinematic Story (1,438), Action Sequence (376), and Anime & Animation (308).

6. **Objective Classification:** Templates can belong to multiple objectives. Build Brand Awareness (2,299) and Promote Product (1,592) are the most common objectives.

7. **Gap Analysis:** Some landing page niches have limited inventory (real-estate: 1, finance: 4, general-business: 4, wellness-fitness: 5). Consider adding more targeted templates in these areas.
