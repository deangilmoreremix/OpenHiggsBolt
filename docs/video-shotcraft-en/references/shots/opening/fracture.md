name: fracture
one_liner: 5×5 tiles reassemble ring by ring in a centre ripple from a 3D shard state into a whole poster, hold a beat with the text lit up, then all the shards spin away from the centre accelerating out of frame
use_when: First shot of an opening, a "from chaos to form" brand/poster reveal; played in reverse or taking only the second half, it can serve as a hard transition
length: about 5.2s (156f@30fps; assembly 0–2.6s · hold 1s · scatter 1.5s)
energy: high (high energy at both ends, still in the middle; good for landing on BGM accents at the start and end)

## Intent
Shoot "the product taking shape" as a physical event: shards fly back to their places from three-dimensional space, each with its own rotation, and the viewer watches a complete image grow out of disorder; after the hold for reading the text, the whole thing explodes out of frame, cleanly handing attention to the next shot. Entrance and exit are symmetric, so one piece of footage can be used forwards and backwards.

## Motion core
- Each of the 25 tiles holds a deterministic pseudo-random initial pose: translation ±450/±300/±490px (x/y/z) + ±180° rotation on three axes, generated by `rand(seed)`, reproducible across renders
- Entrance staggers by Manhattan-distance ripple: `delay = (|r-2|+|c-2|) * 0.045`, the centre settles first and the corners last; each tile runs for 0.34 on `bezier(0.42,0,0.3,1)` (long deceleration before landing); ~10f after landing, a single damped rotateX wobble of ≤2.5° = the "click" of seating into its slot
- The 25 tiles are cut from **the same poster** (each tile takes its own cell, offset by its grid position); only when they're all aligned does the complete image appear; tiles are shaded by orientation (the more side-on to the camera, the darker)
- opacity rises quickly to 1 via `tin * 2.5`: the shards are nearly solid even far away, avoiding a "fade-in feel" that would dilute the physicality
- The poster is printed with the eyebrow INTRODUCING + the video-shotcraft wordmark (logo + lowercase wordmark), sliced with the tiles, lighting up at t=0.42–0.52 to hold the eye during the hold segment; on exit it doesn't fade separately: the text and logo are torn away along with the shards; during the hold segment the whole surface pushes in very slowly by 3%
- The exit doesn't fade: each tile flies 620–880px in the direction away from the frame centre (computed with `atan2` + ±0.8 jitter) with an added spin of ±150°, `inCubic` accelerating, leaving the frame as a solid object

## Parameters
| Parameter | Typical value | Tuning feel |
|------|--------|----------|
| Grid | 5×5, 380×230px, perspective 900px | More tiles make the ripple finer but lower each tile's presence; persp <700 exaggerates the perspective and easily gives the game away |
| Initial scatter | dx±450 / dy±300 / dz −210~+490 | A wider scatter makes the opening more chaotic, but the assembly paths get longer and the delay window must be enlarged to match |
| Ripple interval | 0.045/ring (Manhattan distance) | >0.07 the ripple turns into "calling the roll ring by ring"; <0.03 everything lands nearly at once and the ripple is lost |
| Entrance duration | 0.34 per tile (inOutCubic) | Shorter makes it snap into shape more; pair with the ripple interval so the last tile lands by ≤0.6 |
| Exit window | Take off at 0.70 + delay×0.5, duration 0.18 (inCubic) | **The window must close before t≈0.97**: once, 0.72+delay×0.7/0.26 caused corner tiles to not fully leave the frame, stuck at the frame edge for a second (ruling 2026-08-05) |
| Exit distance | 620–880px (away from centre + random jitter) | Too short a distance leaves shards at the frame edge; the centre tile's direction is random (there is no "away" for it) |
| Poster colours | `ACCENT_HUE=218` derives the whole poster (cool-toned dark field + soft light top-left + glowing horizon arc at the bottom) | Change one hue constant and the whole surface changes colour; seams are 0.5 design px, wider and they cut strokes out of the letters |

## Known pitfalls
- The exit uses translation out of frame rather than an opacity fade: a fade reads as "disappearing" rather than "flying away" and all physicality is lost; when changing parameters keep `opacity = min(1, tin*2.5)` with no exit term attached
- All random values must go through deterministic `rand(seed)` (equivalent to Remotion `random()`); true randomness jitters between renders
- The hold segment (t≈0.6–0.7) is the only window for reading the text; if you lengthen the copy, widen the gap between the label fade-out and the first tile's take-off to match, otherwise the tiles scatter before the text has been read
- Don't let the logo straddle a seam: a seam cuts a thin slit/ragged edge into the logo, which reads as a broken image. The demo aligns the wordmark's centre with the centre of the 3rd row of tiles and sets the font size to 22 so the logo falls entirely within the column 2 tile; when the shards scatter, the logo flies off intact with one tile; recompute whenever you change the logo size or the grid
- When used as a transition, it's recommended to take only the t=0.7–1 scatter segment, half reversed and half forward; mapping the material from each side onto the tile faces needs separate texture mapping
